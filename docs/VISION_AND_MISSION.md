# Stadtstack vision and mission

## Mission

Stadtstack gives a community and its municipality one understandable,
verifiable path from public discussion to reviewed public information and an
explicit city-owned decision. Agents may connect evidence, draft
explanations, and help each role; they do not acquire civic authority by
acting quickly.

## Vision

Communities can exchange reviewed solutions and time-critical public
information over an open signed protocol. A companion can discover that
another municipality faced a similar question, show provenance and local
differences, and help people coordinate without merging private records or
pretending another city's decision automatically applies locally.

The exchange lane can later support multi-municipality incidents such as
wildfire, severe weather, transport disruption, or shared infrastructure. An
alert still needs a named source, jurisdiction, validity window, correction
path, and local authority; openness improves reach and reuse but does not make
every message official.

## Product promise

For one Civic case, a person should be able to see:

1. which public Discussion started the concern;
2. how it became a non-authoritative Suggestion;
3. which Department packages were answered and reviewed;
4. which evidence and redactions support the Reviewed citizen brief;
5. what advisory participation and council deliberation say;
6. which human, city system, or Agent contribution made each statement; and
7. whether any formal city-owned transition actually occurred.

## Ownership model

- **A community product** owns public discussion, community identity,
  participation experience, and city-specific presentation.
- **Stadtstack** owns the municipality-neutral Case journal, review contracts,
  role projections, evidence checksums, and public-exchange contract.
- **An administration workspace** such as OpenDesk or a Buzz-like product owns
  staff documents and human work, not Case truth or authority.
- **A city source system** owns formal proposals, decisions, publications, and
  votes.
- **Nostr** carries signed permanently public discussion and reviewed exchange
  records; it is not private administration storage or municipal authority.
- **Mecky runtimes** are replaceable role-scoped workers. An agent identity is
  provenance, not a staff credential, review attestation, or council mandate.

## Delivery doctrine

1. Prove the whole non-authoritative flow for one synthetic city.
2. Prove durable replay, role isolation, redaction, browser state, and exact
   deletion in a private disposable runtime.
3. Replace reference surfaces with owned community and administration
   Adapters.
4. Integrate formal city actions only through separately authorized city-owned
   Interfaces.
5. Add a second community only after the one-city evidence contract is stable.
6. Add regional incident exchange only after source authority, freshness,
   correction, and jurisdiction rules are explicit.

## Non-goals

Stadtstack does not become a social network, office suite, council system,
voting product, identity provider, municipal database, general-purpose agent
platform, or operations authority. It connects those products through small,
versioned Interfaces while keeping private evidence and formal authority with
their owners.
